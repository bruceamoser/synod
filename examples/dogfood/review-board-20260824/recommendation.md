# Recommendation — review-board

**Verdict:** Adopt private-by-default as the governing default for closed runs (sealed ruling r-001): the raw record of a closed run is held under access control, and publication at closure is limited to the curated published recommendation carrying its grounds. This attaches four conditions: (1) the raw record is retained intact under access control with a defined retention window and a revocation path, curated but never destroyed, summarized away, or made unrecoverable; (2) the published recommendation must carry its underlying grounds; (3) access to the raw record is bounded by need-to-know with an auditable access path; (4) any recommendation reintroducing org-wide readability of raw entries must first demonstrate that the composition-time position-gaming and failure-open exposure harms are eliminated.

## Rulings applied

- r-001 (t-01): The council must adopt private-by-default as the governing default: the raw record of a closed run is held under access control, and publication at closure is limited to the curated published recommendation carrying its grounds. The public-by-default position does not stand as presented, because its two round-2 defenses — a 7.3-compliant redaction and access-control mechanism (f-006) and the appeal-path/reviewability reading (f-006) — answer neither the composition-time position-gaming mechanism (f-003, f-007), which fires before any gate operates on the record, nor the failure-open exposure of the defined public pole (f-004, f-008), for which the support side's only reply invokes a third mechanism the defined poles do not contain. The council's recommendation must be framed within the private default and may not resurrect the public default as defined.
