Should Synod council deliberations be public by default, or private by default?

Public by default: once a run closes, its full record (member findings, arguments, citations, digests) is readable by any organization member.

Private by default: only the orchestrator and named members can read a run until the orchestrator publishes the final recommendation.md to the organization. The raw record is never published.

Weigh: accountability and legitimacy; the chilling effect on dissent (position-gaming); and exposure of sensitive content (unreleased system names, people, security-relevant architecture).
