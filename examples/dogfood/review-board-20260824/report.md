## Summary

Adopt private-by-default as the governing default for closed runs (sealed ruling r-001): the raw record of a closed run is held under access control, and publication at closure is limited to the curated published recommendation carrying its grounds. This attaches four conditions: (1) the raw record is retained intact under access control with a defined retention window and a revocation path, curated but never destroyed, summarized away, or made unrecoverable; (2) the published recommendation must carry its underlying grounds; (3) access to the raw record is bounded by need-to-know with an auditable access path; (4) any recommendation reintroducing org-wide readability of raw entries must first demonstrate that the composition-time position-gaming and failure-open exposure harms are eliminated.
